# Stationary device acceptance checklist

Status: **not_run**. No physical phone was connected when preparing this kit.

## Before the session

Use a named Android phone while stationary, with a sighted operator controlling
the test. Record model/OS, installed APK SHA-256, build/source receipt, language,
permissions, orientation/mount and test time. Keep walking guidance disabled,
generic geometry shadow-only and external SOS actions outside this experiment.

| TalkBack | Traverse inspection and exit at 100% and 200% text scale | Focus order, names, states, touch targets and return focus | not_run |
| Repeated screen exit | Open/close inspection five times | Camera/worker release, no ANR, subsequent reopen and memory observations | not_run |
| Sustained stationary use | Five minutes without walking | Capture/decision ages, rejected data, memory, thermal state, model counts | not_run |

For every row save pass/fail/not_run, exact steps, logs, observed symptom and
recording filename. Missing evidence remains not_run. Stop the session on ANR,
thermal issues, misleading readiness or loss of control; retain partial logs.

## Separate future gate

Controlled walking needs an independently approved protocol, appropriate human
supervision and physical perception evidence. Completing this stationary list
does not grant movement authority or establish model correctness.
